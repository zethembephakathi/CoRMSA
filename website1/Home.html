Foundation That Helps Refugees Settle

The Consortium for Refugees and Migrants in South Africa (CoRMSA), is a national network comprising of twenty-six (26) member organisations in good standing.
It has over twenty years of experience working on migration and refugee protection engaging in advocacy and lobbying, including policy submissions, coordination and network building, capacity building, community engagement and dialogue,
rights awareness and information sharing. Our member organisations include legal practitioners, community-based refugees and migrant-led organisations, advice offices, academic institutions and social service providers amongst others.

Services we offer

Many services are available to help refugees as they resettle and integrate into South Africa.

Asylum Seeker Permit

This permit allows individuals to stay in a country while their asylum application is being processed. It provides temporary legal status and protection against deportation.
Refugees with the appropriate status often require a work permit to legally seek employment in their host country.

Housing and Accommodation

Organizations and government agencies often assist refugees in finding safe and affordable housing, either through temporary shelters, public housing programs, or support in finding private rental housing.

Education and Job Training

Services are offered to help refugees access education, job training, and employment opportunities, enabling them to become self-sufficient and contribute to their new communities.

Our Hubs

Refugee Hubs that offer various amenities and services to refugees to support their well-being and integration into a new community

Legal Aid Center

This Hub offers legal assistance to refugees, helping them understand their rights, navigate immigration processes,
and access legal representation if needed

Childcare Centre

This Hub offers Services that support refugee families with young children by offering daycare,
early childhood education, and parenting support.

Education Centres

This Hub offers Schools and educational programs for refugee children and adults.
This hub is crucial for language acquisition, skill development,
and overall integration into the education system of South Africa
