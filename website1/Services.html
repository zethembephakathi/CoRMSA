Asylum Seeker Permits

Permits Offered

Asylum Status
Asylum is a form of protection for individuals who have fled their home country due to fear of persecution based on race, religion, nationality, membership in a particular social group, or political opinion. Asylum status is typically granted to those who meet the criteria for refugee status

Refugee Status
Refugees are individuals who have been forced to flee their home countries due to well-founded fears of persecution. Refugee form is often granted to those who meet the criteria set out in international refugee law, such as the 1951 Refugee Convention.

Driver Permit
Driver permit form of protection for individuals who will need to work as drivers or own their own Motors.

Business Permit
Business Permit is a form that gives right to sell that is been approved by the health department.

Housing and Accommodation

Refugee Camps

Tents or Temporary Shelters: In emergency situations, refugees may initially be accommodated in temporary shelters, such as tents, to provide immediate relief. Over time, these shelters may be replaced with more durable structures.

Transit Centers

Transit centers are temporary facilities where refugees can stay briefly upon arrival in a host country. Here, they undergo initial processing, including registration and health screenings, before being moved to more permanent housing.

Reception Centers

Temporary Housing: Reception centers are designed to provide temporary housing while refugees await more permanent solutions. They often offer essential services such as medical care, counseling, and orientation to the host country.

Shared Housing

Shared Accommodation: Refugees may share housing with other families or individuals. This could involve sharing an apartment, house, or communal living spaces, fostering a sense of community and support.

Host Family Arrangements

Living with Host Families: Some refugees are accommodated by host families who offer housing and support, providing a more personal and familial environment.

Education and Job Training

Education for Children

Formal Education: Providing refugee children with access to formal education is essential for their intellectual development and integration into the host community. Schools help children learn the local language, adapt to the new culture, and build relationships with peers.

Language Learning

Language Training: Language skills are fundamental for refugees to communicate effectively and access educational and employment opportunities. Language training programs help refugees become proficient in the local language, enhancing their ability to engage in their new environment.

Adult Education

Language and Literacy Programs: Adult education initiatives focus on language and literacy skills for refugees who did not have the opportunity for formal education in their home countries. These programs contribute to improved communication and increased employability.

Vocational Training

Skill Development: Vocational training equips refugees with practical skills aligned with the job market. It can include courses in trades, technology, healthcare, and other sectors, fostering economic independence and enhancing employability.

Entrepreneurship Programs

Business Development Support: For refugees interested in entrepreneurship, programs that offer business training, mentorship, and financial assistance can help them establish and grow their businesses, contributing to local economies.

Our Hubs

Refugee Hubs that offer various amenities and services to refugees to support their well-being and integration into a new community

Legal Aid Center

A Legal Aid Center for refugees serves as a critical support system, offering invaluable assistance in navigating complex legal processes.
These centers guide refugees through asylum applications, providing help with paperwork, legal representation during interviews,
and support in appeals if necessary. They educate refugees on their rights and responsibilities, offering language assistance
and ensuring understanding of legal documents. Additionally, Legal Aid Centers assist with family reunification, documentation,
and obtaining necessary permits. By addressing issues such as exploitation and discrimination, these centers play a key role in protecting
the rights of refugees. Through community outreach and empowerment initiatives, Legal Aid Centers empower refugees to navigate legal systems,
fostering a path to successful resettlement and the rebuilding of their lives in a new country.

Childcare Centre

A Childcare Centre for refugees provides a crucial supportive environment for families by offering childcare services tailored to the unique needs of displaced children. These centers create a safe and nurturing space where refugee children can engage in educational activities, socialize with peers, and receive emotional support. By addressing the childcare needs of refugee families, these centers not only enable parents to focus on rebuilding their lives but also contribute to the overall well-being and development of the next generation. Childcare Centers often integrate educational programs, language learning activities, and cultural enrichment, fostering a sense of stability and normalcy for refugee children amid the challenges of displacement. Through compassionate care and educational initiatives, these centers play a vital role in helping refugee families integrate into their new communities while providing essential support for children's growth and development.

Education Centres

Education centers for refugees play a pivotal role in facilitating the integration and empowerment of displaced individuals by providing essential educational resources and opportunities. These centers offer language classes, academic programs, and vocational training to enhance refugees' skills and knowledge, fostering their ability to adapt to their new environment. By addressing educational needs, these centers contribute to refugees' self-sufficiency, helping them build a foundation for a better future. Education centers also serve as crucial spaces for cultural exchange and community building, promoting a sense of belonging among refugees. Through inclusive and accessible learning initiatives, these centers empower displaced individuals to overcome barriers, pursue academic and vocational goals, and actively participate in the social and economic life of their host communities.
